# Show miles, keep world data in metres

The game's setting reads American (Route 40, county roads, a tow truck "for forty miles"), so everything the player sees is in miles, feet and mph: the dash, road signs, what locals say, the tow card and the load screen. The world data and all code stay in metres. Converting only at the point of display kept the change small and left the authored world, physics and tools untouched. Road signs round to whole or half miles ("ASHFORD 2½").
